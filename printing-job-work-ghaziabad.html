Buying guide

How to brief a printing job

01

Describe the item

Tell us what will be printed on: paper, card, fabric, bags or another item, and its size.

02

Share artwork and colours

Send the design and list the colours. Each colour in screen printing is set up separately.

03

Confirm quantity and deadline

Short and long runs are planned differently, so give us both numbers upfront.

04

Approve a sample

A proof or sample before the full run protects you from surprises.

What is printing job work?

Printing job work means we print to your specification: you supply the artwork or idea, and we handle setup, printing and finishing for your quantity and deadline.

What kinds of job work do you take in Ghaziabad?

Screen printing, serigraphy, flatbed screen printing, stencil printing, custom textile and garment designs, and ID cards.

Do you print on fabric and garments?

Yes. We screen print custom textile designs on garments and apparel. Share the item and quantity for advice.

Yes. We share a proof or sample for approval before bulk printing begins.
