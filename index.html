How it works

Four calm steps to your perfect card.

  1. Visit or message

    Drop in at the shop near Chopla Mandir or send us a WhatsApp with your dates and rough quantity.

  2. Choose your design

    Browse the collection, feel the papers and finishes, and shortlist what suits the occasion.

  3. Personalise and proof

    We set your names, dates, venue, colours and fonts, then share a proof for you to approve.

  4. Print and deliver

    Collect from the shop, get it home delivered in Delhi NCR, or have it posted anywhere in India.

All FAQs
How early should we order our wedding cards?

Ideally six to eight weeks before the wedding. That leaves time to choose, approve the proof, print and send cards out comfortably. Shorter timelines are often possible, so do ask.

Can you print urgent orders?

Yes, whenever our existing commitments allow. Call or WhatsApp us with your date and quantity and we will tell you honestly what we can manage.

What can be personalised on a card?

Names, wedding dates, timings, venue details, printing colours and fonts. The card design itself stays as you chose it from the collection.

Do you deliver outside Ghaziabad?

We offer personal home delivery across Delhi NCR and postal shipping to anywhere in India.
