Buying guide

How to choose the right wedding card

01

Start with your guest list

Count the families you will invite and add ten percent for last minute additions. Quantity affects which designs and printing methods make the most sense for your budget.

02

Match the card to the wedding

A grand evening wedding suits royal wine and foil cards, while daytime or destination weddings often look best on floral or minimal designs. Bring your theme colours to the store and we will shortlist options.

03

Decide on wording and inserts

Choose Hindi, English or both, and list every function that needs an insert. Getting this right before the proof saves time later.

04

Leave time for the proof

Read every name, date and timing on the proof carefully. Printing starts only after you approve it, so plan six to eight weeks ahead where you can.

All FAQs
How early should we order wedding cards in Ghaziabad?

Six to eight weeks before the wedding is ideal. It leaves time to choose, approve the proof, print and send cards out comfortably. Shorter timelines are often possible, so do ask.

Can the wedding card wording be in Hindi?

Yes. We set invitations in Hindi, English or both, and can help with traditional wording if you are unsure what to write.

What can be personalised on a wedding card?

Names, wedding dates, timings, venue details, printing colours and fonts. The card design itself stays as you selected it.

Can you print inserts for other functions?

Yes. Inserts for mehendi, haldi, sangeet and reception can be printed to match your main card.

What is the price of wedding cards?

Prices depend on the design, paper, quantity and printing. Send us the design name or a photo on WhatsApp and we will share options that suit your budget.

Do you deliver wedding cards outside Ghaziabad?

Yes. Personal home delivery across Delhi NCR and postal shipping to anywhere in India.
