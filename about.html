Our story

"A wedding invitation is the first glimpse of a celebration. It reaches the home before the guests reach the venue."

शुभ विवाह Raj&Riya20 . 12 . 2026
Together with their families Aarav&Meera request the pleasure of your company20 . 12 . 2026
1976Founded

Virendra Cards began in 1976, when our grandfather set up a small card counter near Chopla Mandir. He chose every design himself, knew most of his customers by name, and believed that the card a family sends out says something about how much the occasion means to them.

Our father grew up between stacks of paper and the hum of the press. He kept the same promise while the craft changed around him, adding new printing methods, box invitations and finishes that families began to ask for, without losing the personal attention that brought people back.

Today the third generation runs the shop. We have added modern designs, WhatsApp proofs and delivery across Delhi NCR, but the heart of the business is the same: you sit with us, you touch the paper, you see your names set properly, and you leave knowing the cards will be right.

Many families who ordered their own wedding cards from us now return for their children's. That trust is the thing we are proudest of, and the thing we work hardest to keep.

Business details

Virendra Cards, Naya Ganj, Ghaziabad.

Location and overview

Established in 1976, Virendra Cards is one of the best known names for wedding cards and printing in Naya Ganj, Ghaziabad. Families from across the city, and from Delhi NCR, come to our counter near Chopla Mandir for invitations, while local shops and businesses rely on our press for everyday printing.

The store sits in Agrasen Bazaar, a short walk from Chopla Mandir, so first-time visitors find us easily and the market is well connected by local transport. Over five decades the business has grown on one simple belief: customer satisfaction matters as much as the product. That is why so many families return to us for every celebration.

Products and services offered

Wedding, engagement and box invitation cards with personalised printing, together with a full range of printing work. Our staff are happy to answer questions, show samples and guide you to the right choice.
